Frequently Asked Questions

Do you manufacture directly from customer drawings?

Yes. We manufacture custom components based on customer drawings and review relevant manufacturing considerations before production where needed.

Can you review manufacturing considerations before sampling?

Yes. We can review manufacturing considerations related to geometry, tolerances, materials, or production before sampling.

Do you support prototype and repeat production?

Yes. We support prototype validation and repeat production, with manufacturing and inspection considerations reviewed as the project moves between stages.

What materials can you work with?

Carbon steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, brass and aluminum are commonly supported depending on the drawing requirements.

What information should I provide with a drawing?

Helpful information includes the drawing, material, estimated quantity, critical tolerances, surface requirements, and any important application or assembly requirements. A 3D model can also be provided if available.

Can you provide inspection reports?

Inspection reports and material documentation can be provided where applicable to the project requirements.
