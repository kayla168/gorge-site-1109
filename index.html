WHAT WE MAKE

Custom fasteners used where equipment is opened, maintained and re-installed.

We focus on the fastening parts used in parts washers and industrial cleaning machines where access, re-installation and long-term reliability matter. These are usually small components — but when fit, retention or vibration performance is poor, they can lead to downtime, repeated rework and slower service turnaround.

More than just one screw.
Some of the most useful service-friendly parts are fastening assemblies with spacing, sealing, retention or stack-up logic built into the part set. This is where installation consistency, repeatable re-assembly and easier maintenance often start.

Custom pins

Positioning and repeatable alignment during assembly and re-installation
  • Straight pins, stepped pins, shoulder pins
  • Chamfers / lead-ins to avoid jamming during service work
  • Hardened working zones when needed
  • Ground OD or controlled fit when needed

Special screws & bolts

Fastening parts for covers, access points and assemblies opened during maintenance
  • Captive designs to prevent dropped fasteners
  • Anti-loosening concepts for vibration-prone areas
  • Thread forms selected for repeated use
  • Compatible with standard tools (hex / Torx / socket)

Sleeves & bushings

Alignment, fit control and wear management inside cleaning systems
  • Controlled ID for stable assembly
  • Press-fit or slip-fit options depending on service needs
  • Chamfers / lead-ins to avoid assembly jams
  • Suitable for wet / chemical environments

Spacers & washers

Thickness control for consistent assembly and rebuild work
  • Thickness-controlled spacers and shims
  • Batch-matched sets for rebuilds
  • Flatness and parallelism on request
  • Metric & inch options

Installation collars, standoffs & small adapters

Low-complexity custom parts used around machine sub-assemblies
  • Collars, standoffs, small adapters
  • Repeatable geometry for maintenance cycles
  • Used where standard hardware is not an ideal fit
  • Material traceability available when required

Captive & service-friendly fastening parts

  • Captive screw concepts for covers opened during maintenance
  • Retention features to reduce dropped small parts
  • Practical designs for repeated maintenance cycles
