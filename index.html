WHAT WE MAKE

Custom fasteners used where equipment is opened, serviced and re-installed.

We focus on the simple but critical fastening parts used in parts washers and industrial cleaning machines — especially in areas affected by vibration, frequent access and maintenance work. These are not main structural components, but they often decide whether a service repair is quick — or turns into downtime, rework, and delayed shipments.

Custom pins

Positioning & alignment during assembly and re-installation
  • Straight pins, stepped pins, shoulder pins
  • Chamfers / lead-ins to avoid jamming during service work
  • Hardened working zones when needed
  • Ground OD or controlled fit when needed

Special screws & bolts

Fastening solutions for access panels and service covers
  • Captive designs to prevent dropped fasteners
  • Anti-loosening concepts for vibration-prone areas
  • Thread forms selected for repeated use
  • Compatible with standard tools (hex / Torx / socket)

Sleeves & bushings

Alignment & wear control inside cleaning systems
  • Controlled ID for stable assembly
  • Press-fit or slip-fit options depending on service needs
  • Chamfers / lead-ins to avoid assembly jams
  • Suitable for wet / chemical environments

Spacers & washers

Pack-up control for consistent re-assembly
  • Thickness-controlled spacers and shims
  • Batch-matched sets for rebuilds
  • Flatness and parallelism on request
  • Metric & inch options

Simple turned components

Low-complexity parts used across machine sub-assemblies
  • Collars, standoffs, small adapters
  • Repeatable geometry for maintenance cycles
  • Ideal for replacement and aftermarket use
  • Material traceability available when required

Service & replacement support

When standard fasteners don’t fit your application
  • Low volume, drawing or sample based
  • Focused on maintenance & service needs
  • Designed to complement your existing BOM
