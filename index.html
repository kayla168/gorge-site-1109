WHAT WE MAKE

Small-batch custom parts your team doesn’t want to tie up the CNC with.

We focus on the simple but critical pieces that keep fixtures, tools and small automation modules together — so your in-house machines can stay on the complex, high-value work.

Custom pins

Locating · alignment · stop pins
  • Straight pins, stepped pins, shoulder pins
  • Chamfered or radiused lead-in for easy insertion
  • Hardened working zones when needed
  • OD grinding and surface finish on request

Shafts & axles

Support, rotation & guide shafts
  • Through-hardened or surface-hardened options
  • Keyways, grooves, threads, cross-holes
  • Length control for stack-up consistency
  • Suited for bushings, bearings or direct fit

Sleeves & bushings

Alignment & wear control
  • Bored ID with controlled clearance
  • Press-fit, slip-fit or H7/g6 design
  • Chamfers / lead-ins to avoid assembly jams
  • Suitable for fixtures, jigs and small modules

Spacers & washers

Pack-up & gap control
  • Thickness-controlled spacers and shims
  • Flatness and parallelism on request
  • Batch-matched sets for rebuilds
  • Fits for metric & inch fasteners

Simple turned components

Overflow for small, repetitive parts
  • Low-complexity parts that burn shop time
  • Bosses, collars, standoffs, small adapters
  • Ideal for repeat fixture and tooling builds
  • Material traceability when needed
